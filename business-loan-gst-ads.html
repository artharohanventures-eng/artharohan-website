Common questions

How fast will you reply?

On WhatsApp within 1 hour, between 9 AM and 9 PM, every day of the week. If you message after 9 PM, we reply the next morning.

Do I need to give property or any security?

No. Unsecured business loans do not need property or other collateral. Lenders still check your GST returns, bank statements and credit history.

Will this affect my CIBIL score?

No. We do not check your CIBIL score here. A lender checks it only when you decide to apply with them.

I am not GST registered, or my business is under 1 year old.

Most lenders need both. Message us anyway and we tell you honestly what is possible now, or how long to wait.

Are you a bank or a lender?

No. Artharohan Ventures is a guidance and facilitation service. The loan is sanctioned and given by the lender, after their own credit check.
