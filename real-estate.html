Resale or new construction in West Delhi: what actually changes.

A detailed, numbers-first guide. Stamp duty, TDS, title verification, and a real worked example. No listings, no pitch.

8 minute read · Last Updated: July 2026 · Written by Artharohan Ventures
Quick Summary
  • Need immediate possession? → Resale
  • Budget matters more than timeline? → New Construction (lower entry price, but GST applies)
  • Want to avoid legal paperwork risk? → New Construction from a reputed, RERA-registered builder

A fact most buyers don't know: Delhi's stamp duty is 6% for a male buyer, 4% for a female buyer, and 5% for joint male-female ownership, calculated on whichever is higher, the sale price or the government circle rate. Registering the property in a woman's name (solely or jointly) is a real, legal way to reduce this cost, worth considering before you finalise how the sale deed is structured.
