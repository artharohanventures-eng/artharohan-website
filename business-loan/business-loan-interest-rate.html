The rate is not the full cost

A processing fee of 1% to 2% plus GST is taken by the lender. There can also be charges for part-payment, foreclosure and late payment. Ask for every charge in writing, and ask whether the quoted rate is flat or reducing.

Rs 10 lakh over 36 months

An example, not an offer. The lender sets the final terms.

On a reducing balance, the EMI is about Rs 34,178 at 14% and about Rs 36,152 at 18%. If the same 14% is a flat rate, the interest is charged on the full Rs 10 lakh for all three years, and the EMI becomes about Rs 39,444. A 1% to 2% processing fee is Rs 10,000 to Rs 20,000, plus GST on the fee.

Can you get a lower rate?

Often a little. Improve your credit score before you apply, file GST on time, show steady bank credits, and compare two or three lenders instead of taking the first offer. Rates sit in bands, so do not expect a big cut. If your GST returns are pending, read business loan when GST returns are not filed first.

Business loan interest rate, answered honestly

What is the interest rate on an unsecured business loan in Delhi?

The lenders we work with typically quote 14% to 18% a year for a GST-registered business with a decent credit score. The lender sets the final rate after seeing your papers.

What decides my business loan interest rate?

The owner's credit score decides the band most. Business age, clean GST returns, steady bank statements, the loan amount and tenure, and the lender you choose also move it.

Is the interest rate the full cost of the loan?

No. A processing fee of 1% to 2% plus GST is taken by the lender, and there can be charges for part-payment, foreclosure and late payment. Compare the total cost, not only the rate.

What is the difference between a flat rate and a reducing rate?

A flat rate is charged on the full loan amount for the whole tenure. A reducing rate is charged only on what you still owe. A flat rate costs much more than it sounds, so ask which one a quote is.

Can I get a lower rate than the first one quoted?

Often a little. Improve your credit score before applying, file GST on time, show steady bank credits, and compare two or three lenders instead of taking the first offer. Rates sit in bands, so do not expect a big cut.
