Business loan reject ho gaya, sabse pehle kya karein?

Sabse pehle lender se reject ka exact reason poochhein. Wajah pata chalegi tabhi sahi fix ho payega. Bina wajah jaane dobara ya doosre lender ko apply karna wahi galti dohrana hai.

Reject hone ke baad kitne din wait karna chahiye?

Koi fixed time nahi hai, yeh reason par depend karta hai. Agar wajah koi kaagaz tha, to theek karke jaldi dobara apply kar sakte hain. Agar wajah credit record ya business ki umar thi, to fix hone mein kuch mahine lag sakte hain.

Ek lender ne mana kiya, kya doosra lender approve kar sakta hai?

Haan, kar sakta hai, kyunki har lender ki policy alag hoti hai. Ek lender jo file mana kare, doosre ke rules mein fit ho sakti hai. Lekin jo kamzori sab lenders ko dikhti hai, wo pehle theek karni padegi.

Business loan sabse zyada kis wajah se reject hota hai?

Hamari files mein sabse zyada wajah low CIBIL score ya overdue payments hai. Business ka 1 saal se kam purana hona bhi eligibility ki badi rukawat hai.
