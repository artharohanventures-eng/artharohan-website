One year with GST can be enough to start

Unsecured business loans are available for GST-registered businesses that have been running for at least a year. A business that has just completed its first year can qualify, as long as its papers are clean. What it cannot do is rely on age alone: the lender still decides from your file.

In our files, the lenders we work with typically look for around Rs 10 lakh in monthly sales and the last 12 months of GST returns. Some ask for more and some for less.

What a lender checks first on a new business

With only a year of history, there is not much else to go on, so the returns carry the file.

12 months of GST returns

Filed regularly since registration, with no gaps. This is the first thing we see lenders look at for a business that is a year old.

Sales against the lender's minimum

The returns also show whether your monthly sales reach what the lender looks for.

The rest of the file

Bank statements and credit history are read next to the returns. For the full list, see the GST-based business loan guide.

Why files from 1-year-old businesses get turned down

The most common reason we see is asking for too big an amount. A short track record supports a smaller loan more easily than a large one. When the request is out of proportion to the business, the file weakens even if everything else is in order.

To work out an amount that fits, read how much business loan you can get on turnover. Already turned down once? See what to do after a rejection.

What to do before you apply

File your GST returns on time, every month. It is the simplest way to make a new business's file stronger, and it costs nothing. A clean, regular run of returns is what the lender will read first.

The documents are the same as any GST business

Being new does not add extra papers.

There is no separate document list for a business that is a year old. Use the standard list in the GST-based business loan guide, and send us your slip if you want a lender check on WhatsApp.

1-year-old business loan questions, answered honestly

Can a 1-year-old business get a business loan?

Yes, it can. Unsecured business loans are available for GST-registered businesses that have been running for at least a year. A business that has just completed one year can qualify if its papers are clean, but whether a lender approves depends on the file.

What do lenders check first for a 1-year-old business?

Regular GST filing for the last 12 months. For a business that is new, a clean run of returns with no gaps is the first thing a lender looks at.

Why do 1-year-old businesses get turned down?

The most common reason in our files is asking for too big an amount. A short track record supports a smaller loan more easily than a large one, so a request out of proportion to the business weakens the file.

What should I do before I apply?

File your GST returns on time, every month. A regular record of filings is the simplest thing you can do to make your file stronger, and it costs nothing.

Do I need extra documents because my business is new?

No. The documents are the same as for any GST-registered business. See the GST-based business loan guide for the list.

Does the lender need a minimum monthly sales figure?

Lenders do look at sales. In our files, the lenders we work with typically look for around Rs 10 lakh in monthly sales for an unsecured business loan, though some ask for more and some for less.
