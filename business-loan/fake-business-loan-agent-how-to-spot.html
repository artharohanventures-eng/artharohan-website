The first red flag: money before approval

If anyone asks for money before your loan is approved, stop. It does not matter what they call it: file charge, approval fee, registration or a deposit to “unlock” the loan. A lender decides on your documents and credit profile, not on a payment to an agent.

What a real processing fee looks like

Genuine lenders do charge a processing fee. The difference is when and where it is paid.

When

At sanction, once the lender has approved your loan. Not before.

Where

Into the lender's own account, with a receipt. Not to a person's account or a personal UPI ID.

What you get

A sanction letter in the lender's name that shows the fee. Check the amount against it before you pay.

Other warning signs

  • A promise of sure approval, with no documents or no credit check
  • The agent will not tell you which lender will give the loan
  • Pressure to pay today or lose the offer
  • A request for your OTP or any password
  • A payment link or account that is not in the lender's name

No lender can honestly guarantee an approval before it has seen your documents.

How to check a lender or agent: three checks together

  1. Ask the lender directly. Use the lender's own official website or helpline, not a number the agent gives you.
  2. Check the registration. A genuine bank or NBFC appears in the RBI's lists of registered entities.
  3. Match the paperwork. The sanction letter and the account that takes the fee must be in the same lender's name.

If you have already paid

Do all three together. The sooner you act, the better your chance of limiting the loss.

  1. Tell your bank at once. Ask it to try to stop or flag the transaction.
  2. Report it. Use the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal at cybercrime.gov.in, or call 1930.
  3. Keep the evidence. Save the chats, receipts and payment screenshots.

Fake loan agent questions, answered honestly

Is it normal to pay a fee before a business loan is approved?

No. Asking for money before approval is the first red flag. A genuine lender takes its processing fee at sanction, into the lender's own account, and gives you a receipt. If someone wants money first to "unlock" or "guarantee" your loan, walk away.

What does a real processing fee look like?

It is charged by the lender at the time of sanction, paid into the lender's own account, and shown on the sanction letter in the lender's name. Typical fees are around 1% to 2% plus GST for the lenders we work with. Check the amount on the sanction letter before you pay.

How do I check that a lender or agent is genuine?

Do three checks together. Ask the lender through its own official website or helpline, not through a number the agent gives you. Check that the bank or NBFC is a registered entity on the RBI's lists. And make sure the sanction letter and the payment account are in the same lender's name.

Do I have to pay anyone to get a Mudra loan?

No. Applying for a Mudra loan is free, and no agent or consultant can charge a fee to process it. If someone asks for an upfront payment, treat it as fraud and report it.

I have already paid someone. What should I do?

Do three things together, as quickly as you can. Tell your bank and ask it to try to stop or flag the transaction. Report it on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930. And keep all the evidence: chats, receipts and payment screenshots.
