Do this first: stop applying elsewhere

A rejection is information. Before you send your file anywhere else, pause new applications and find out why you were turned down. Each full application usually adds a credit enquiry to your report, and several in a short time can make the next lender more cautious.

The most common mistake is not asking why. Owners reapply with the same problem unchanged, and get the same answer. Some lenders explain the reason in detail and some only in general terms. Ask anyway.

Five questions to ask the lender or agent

  1. What was the exact reason? Score, income, documents, existing EMIs, or the type of loan?
  2. Is it a final no, or can I resubmit after fixing it? Some reasons can be fixed in days, others take longer.
  3. Which document or number caused the problem? A mismatch is easier to fix than a weak profile.
  4. Would a smaller amount or a different tenure work? Sometimes the file is fine and the ask is too big.
  5. Is another kind of lender a better fit? A bank, an NBFC and a fintech judge the same file differently.

Match the fix to the reason

How long you wait depends on what went wrong, so there is no fixed number of months. Find your reason below.

Low credit score or overdue accounts

Check your credit report for overdue accounts and mistakes, then clear dues. Time to recover depends on the cause: a few overdue accounts can be cleared quickly, serious defaults take longer. A different lender usually does not help, because it sees the same report.

Too many existing EMIs

Close small loans and card dues first, or ask for a smaller amount. The same file at another lender usually meets the same limit.

GST and bank statements do not match, or filing was irregular

File every pending return and route business sales through the business account, so the two tell the same story. Apply again after several clean months.

Business is too new

Many lenders want 1 year of GST filing. Look at Mudra, which is free to apply for, or wait until you cross the line.

Wrong loan type or wrong lender

This one can change fast. A working capital limit instead of a term loan, or an NBFC instead of a bank, can turn the same file into a yes.

Fix first, or try a different lender?

A fit problem

The wrong product, the wrong kind of lender, or an amount that was too high. A different lender or loan type can say yes without changing your file.

A credit problem

A low score, overdue accounts or heavy EMIs. The file travels with you, so the answer is usually the same. Fix the cause first, then apply once.

Before you apply again

  1. Know the reason clearly. If nobody told you, ask. If the lender will not say, check your credit report and your GST returns for the likely cause.
  2. Fix that one thing. Clear the dues, file the returns, close the small loans, or change the loan type.
  3. Check your numbers again. Credit report, last 12 months of GST returns, and bank deposits next to declared sales.
  4. Pick one lender that fits. Do not send the same file to many lenders at once.
  5. Tell us what happened. Send the slip above and say you were rejected. We tell you honestly what looks possible, and when to come back.

Rejected loan questions, answered honestly

Why was my business loan rejected?

The common reasons are a low credit score or overdue accounts, too many existing EMIs, GST returns that do not match your bank statements, irregular GST filing, a business that is too new, and a loan type or lender that does not fit your profile. Ask the lender for the exact reason before you change anything.

How long should I wait before applying again?

There is no single waiting time. It depends on the reason. A missing document or a fit problem can be sorted quickly, while a credit problem or heavy EMIs need real fixing first. Apply again once the cause is fixed, not on a calendar date.

Will applying to another lender straight away help?

Only if the rejection was a fit problem, such as the wrong loan type or the wrong kind of lender. If the cause was your credit or your existing EMIs, the same file usually meets the same answer elsewhere. Each application can also add a credit enquiry, so pause new applications until you know why.

Does a rejection hurt my credit score?

The application usually leaves a credit enquiry on your report. Several enquiries in a short time can lower your score and make lenders cautious. That is why we suggest stopping new applications while you find out the reason.

Should I pay an agent to get my loan approved?

No. Be careful with anyone who asks for an upfront payment to arrange or fix a loan. A lender decides on your documents and credit profile, not on a fee.
