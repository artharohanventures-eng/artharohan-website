Pick the loan type before you pick a lender

Most Delhi business owners need one of three things. Knowing which one saves you from applying for the wrong product and getting rejected for it. We most often help with loans between Rs 5 lakh and Rs 15 lakh.

Unsecured business loan

A lump sum for stock, expansion, equipment or cash flow, without pledging property. The lender looks at your GST returns, bank statements and credit history instead of collateral.

Fits: shops, traders, wholesalers, service firms, workshops with 1+ year of GST filing.

Working capital loan or overdraft

Money for the gap between buying and getting paid. A cash credit or overdraft charges interest only on the amount you use, for the days you use it. Many lenders ask for security, though unsecured limits exist for strong profiles.

Fits: seasonal traders, distributors, firms with long payment cycles.

Mudra loan (government scheme)

For small and micro non-farm businesses. Shishu up to Rs 50,000, Kishor up to Rs 5 lakh, Tarun up to Rs 10 lakh, and Tarun Plus up to Rs 20 lakh for borrowers who repaid an earlier Tarun loan. Collateral-free.

Fits: newer or smaller businesses, including those under 1 year.

Mudra fraud warning. Applying for a Mudra loan is free. No agent or consultant can charge a fee to “process” it. If anyone asks for an upfront payment, walk away and report it to your bank or the cybercrime portal.

Why business loans get rejected

Most rejections can be seen coming. Fix these before you apply, because each hard enquiry on your credit report can count against you.

Low credit score

Missed payments and overdue accounts shrink your lender options or raise your rate.

Fix: clear overdue dues, check your score, wait 3 to 6 months.

GST and bank statements do not match

If sales in your GST returns and deposits in your bank statements tell different stories, lenders ask questions.

Fix: route business sales through the business account and file GST on time.

Business is too new

Many lenders want 1 year or more of GST filing, and some banks want 2 to 3.

Fix: look at Mudra, or wait until you cross 1 year.

Too many existing EMIs

High monthly obligations reduce what a lender will add on top.

Fix: close small loans and card dues first.

Irregular GST filing

Late or nil returns look like a weak business, even if your sales are real.

Fix: file every month for at least 6 months before applying.

Wrong loan or wrong lender

Applying for a term loan when you need working capital, or to a bank when an NBFC fits your profile, is a rejection you could avoid.

Fix: pick the loan type first, then the lender.

Before you sign any sanction letter, check whether the rate is flat or reducing, the processing fee, prepayment and foreclosure charges, and any insurance bundled in. Compare the total you repay, not just the headline rate.

Business loan questions, answered honestly

Can I get a business loan in Delhi without collateral?

Yes. Unsecured business loans do not need property or other collateral. The lender checks your GST returns, bank statements and credit history instead. If your business is GST-registered and has run for at least 1 year, you have options. The amount and rate depend on the lender.

Which documents are needed for a GST-based business loan in Delhi?

Usually the GST certificate and recent GST returns (GSTR-3B), the last 12 months of business bank statements, the owner's PAN and Aadhaar, and business address proof. Add your Udyam certificate and ITR if you have them. Each lender has its own list, and we tell you exactly which ones you need.

Can I get a business loan in Delhi without ITR?

Some lenders assess a loan on GST returns and bank statements when ITR is missing. Your options are fewer and the rate is often higher. Approval is never guaranteed. Message us your numbers and we tell you what is realistic.

How old should my business be?

Many lenders want at least 1 year of GST-registered business. Some banks ask for 2 to 3 years. If your business is newer, the Mudra scheme may fit, or we tell you how long to wait.

What interest rate and fees should I expect?

Our lenders typically quote 14% to 18% a year on unsecured business loans, a processing fee of 1% to 2% plus GST, and a repayment period of 12 to 60 months. Banks may quote lower for strong, long-standing profiles. The lender sets the final terms after seeing your documents.

What credit score do I need?

Many lenders prefer 700 or above for an unsecured business loan. Some consider lower scores with a higher rate or a smaller amount. The lender makes the final decision.

Why do business loans get rejected?

Common reasons are a low credit score, GST returns that do not match bank statements, a business under 1 year old, high existing EMIs, irregular GST filing, and choosing a loan type or lender that does not fit your profile. Fixing these before you apply improves your chances.
