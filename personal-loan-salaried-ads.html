Representative example
Loan of Rs 1,00,000 for 36 months at 15% a year: monthly EMI Rs 3,467. Interest Rs 24,795. Processing fee Rs 2,360 (2% plus 18% GST). Total cost of the loan Rs 27,155, so you repay Rs 1,27,155 in all.
This is an example only. Your lender's rate and fees depend on your profile and will differ. Processing fee and other charges are set by the lender. We share them with you before you decide.

What lenders usually check for salaried applicants

  • CIBIL score of 700 or more gets the best rates
  • A stable job with regular salary credit in your bank account
  • Your total EMIs, old and new, staying under 40 to 50% of monthly income
  • PAN and Aadhaar, last 3 months salary slips, and bank statements

Common questions

How fast will you reply?

We reply on WhatsApp within 1 hour, between 9 AM and 9 PM, every day of the week. If you message after 9 PM, we reply the next morning.

Will this affect my CIBIL score?

No. We do not check your CIBIL score here. A lender checks it only when you decide to apply with them.

Do I pay you anything?

No. You pay us nothing. If a loan is disbursed through us, the lender may pay us a commission.

How much loan can I get on my salary?

It depends on your total EMIs, your CIBIL score and the lender. Send your details and we tell you a realistic range before you apply anywhere.

Can I get a loan with a low CIBIL score?

Options become limited below 700 and rates are higher. We tell you honestly if waiting a few months or choosing a cheaper option is better.

Are you a bank or a lender?

No. Artharohan Ventures is a guidance and facilitation service. The loan is sanctioned and given by the lender, after their own credit check.
